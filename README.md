# JNVCKM Alumni Association

**<https://jnvckm.org>**

The website of the JNVCKM Alumni Association: former students of PM SHRI
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Chikkamagaluru, at Seegodu near Balehonnur. The
association was formed in 1994.

The site records what the association does:

- **Programs:** IGNITE, where alumni return to guide students in classes 10
  to 12, and the Navodaya Cricket League, an annual tournament between batches.
- **Contributions:** what alumni and batches have funded and built at the
  Vidyalaya, and who they have helped.
- **Events:** the annual campus meet, reunions, workshops, and every IGNITE
  edition and NCL season on record.
- **Stories:** news, reports and memories from alumni.
- **The Vidyalaya:** the school every member comes from.

## Getting involved

Association news reaches alumni through their **batch representative**. To
join in, volunteer, mentor or contribute, start there. The
[Connect page](https://jnvckm.org/connect/) has the other ways to reach the
association.

## Corrections and missing records

The records come from the association's archive. If something is wrong, or an
event, project or photograph is missing, open an issue in this repository with
what you know and where it comes from, such as a date, a poster, a newspaper
clipping or a photograph.

Photographs are published only as records of events. If you appear in one and
want it removed, open an issue to ask for that.

## About this site

- **Fast and private.** Static pages, no trackers, no ads, no cookies.
- **Edited by the association.** Content lives in this repository and is changed
  through a web editor, so every edit is a visible commit.
- **Readable anywhere.** Built for phones first, with accessible contrast and
  headings.

This is not an official website of the school or of Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti.
