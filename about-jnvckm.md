# About Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Chikkamagaluru (JNVCKM)

## Overview

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Chikkamagaluru (JNVCKM) is a co-educational, fully residential school located at Seegodu village near Balehonnur in Koppa Taluk, Chikkamagaluru district, Karnataka. 

Established on **23rd October 1986**, it is one of the senior-most Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas in India. The school began at the Coffee Research Station in Koppa Taluk before shifting to its present permanent campus at Seegodu (about 5 km from Balehonnur), as it expanded over time.

JNVCKM functions under the **Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS)**, Ministry of Education, Government of India. As part of the Navodaya system, its mission is to provide **quality modern education** to talented children, primarily from rural backgrounds, with a strong emphasis on equity, excellence, and national integration.

The school has been designated as a **PM SHRI School**, reflecting its status as a model institution selected for holistic upgradation and academic innovation.

JNVCKM offers classes from **VI to XII**, with **English** as the medium of instruction, and is affiliated to the **Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)**.

---

## Campus & Facilities

The JNVCKM campus covers approximately **15 acres** in a serene rural, coffee-growing region, providing an environment that supports focused learning and all-round development.

Key facilities include:

- **Fully residential arrangement** with separate hostels for boys and girls  
- Well-equipped **classrooms** and **science laboratories**
- **Computer lab** as per NVS norms
- **Library** with reference and reading materials
- **Music and dance rooms** to encourage cultural and artistic talent
- **Gymnasium** and facilities for indoor activities
- Extensive **playgrounds** (around 15,000 m²) for outdoor sports and games
- Indoor facilities for activities such as **table tennis** and **badminton**
- Regular **health check-ups** through visiting medical staff

A range of co-curricular and enrichment activities are integrated into campus life, including clubs and programs such as:

- Literary, science, and arts clubs  
- **Youth Parliament**
- **Scouts & Guides**
- **NCC** and **NSS**
- Cultural, sports, and community outreach activities

---

## Academics & Achievements

JNVCKM follows the **CBSE curriculum** with an emphasis on both scholastic and co-scholastic excellence.

Highlights include:

- Consistently strong **Class X and XII board results**, often with very high or 100% pass percentages
- Encouragement and support for students to appear in **competitive exams** and scholarship tests
- Admissions through the **Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST)** for Class VI, with limited **lateral entry** in Classes IX and XI, ensuring a merit-based and inclusive student community
- Regular participation and recognition at **cluster, regional, and national-level** competitions in academics, sports, arts, and cultural events
- Recognition of staff excellence, including national-level awards for dedicated teachers

Many alumni of JNVCKM have gone on to distinguished careers in higher education, public service, research, industry, and entrepreneurship, reflecting the long-term impact of the Vidyalaya’s academic and value-based foundation.

## Affiliation & Governance

- **Affiliated Board:** Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)  
- **Affiliation Number:** 840012  
- **School Code:** 49034  
- **Since:** Affiliated to CBSE since 1989  

JNVCKM is administered by the **Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti**, an autonomous organization under the Ministry of Education. The school adheres to all CBSE and NVS academic, administrative, and residential norms.

Education, boarding, and lodging are provided **free of cost**, in line with Navodaya policy, with only a nominal fee (as per NVS rules) for certain categories of students in Classes IX–XII. This makes JNVCKM part of a unique national network committed to **accessible, high-quality education** for rural talent.
