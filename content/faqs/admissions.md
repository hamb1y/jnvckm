---
question: "How does JNVCKM select students?"
answer: |
  Admissions happen through the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) for Class VI. A limited number of lateral seats for Classes IX and XI are filled through separate tests conducted by Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti.

  Detailed notifications are published on [navodaya.gov.in](https://navodaya.gov.in) and amplified by the alumni network. Coaching is **not** necessary—focus on NCERT fundamentals.
